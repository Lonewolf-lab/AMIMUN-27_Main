# Features Research

**Domain:** High-End Architectural Builder Showcase  
**Researched:** 2026-09-17  
**Confidence:** HIGH  

## Table Stakes Features

Must-have features standard for luxury home builders:

| Feature | Complexity | Priority | Description |
|---------|------------|----------|-------------|
| **Hero Video / Imagery Showcase** | Low | P0 | Full-bleed, atmospheric imagery/video celebrating architectural craftsmanship in Essendon & surrounding suburbs |
| **Curated Portfolio Gallery** | Medium | P0 | Filterable/navigable case studies (`Ascot Residence`, `Calibre`, `Obsidian`, `Small House`) with high-res photography |
| **Comprehensive Case Study Detail** | Medium | P0 | Detailed specs: architect attribution, project scope, photography carousel, architectural narrative |
| **Architect Collaboration Hub** | Low | P1 | Dedicated `/for-architects` section detailing Haven's tender process, precision estimating, and construction methodology |
| **Expertise & Craftsmanship Tour** | Low | P1 | Detailed breakdown of heritage restorations, custom luxury builds, and complex engineering solutions |
| **Interactive Consultation Form** | Low | P0 | Polished contact inquiry form capturing project address, architectural status, and budget scale |

## Luxury Differentiators

Features that elevate the brand above conventional residential builders:

| Feature | Complexity | Impact | Description |
|---------|------------|--------|-------------|
| **Fluid Typography & Staggered Reveal** | Medium | High | Kinetic typography reveals (`[animation="text-reveal"]`) on scroll entry |
| **Inertia Smooth Scrolling (Lenis)** | Low | High | Luxury bespoke feel with calibrated scroll damping |

| **Interactive Spatial Showcase** | High | High | Embedded 3D / spatial geometry highlights (Three.js / Draco loader integration) |
